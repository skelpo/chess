Privacy

Last updated November 5, 2026

tuichess 3D for iPhone and iPad

tuichess 3D does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet: you play against an engine that runs on your device.

Your settings and the game in progress are saved on your device only, so you can continue where you left off. They are never sent anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

This website

tuichess.dev counts page views with Plausible, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data. If you play online from the terminal app, the server keeps the name you choose and your games and scores for the high-score table.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub, or reach skelpo GmbH through skelpo.com.
