tuichess 3D is chess on a 3D board for iPhone and iPad, against the tuichess engine.

Playing

  • Tap one of your pieces, then one of the marked squares.
  • Drag to turn the camera, pinch to zoom.
  • Menu (top right) pauses the game: take back a move, start a new game, change settings, or resign.
  • Pick your color and the engine's level (1 to 10) under New game.
